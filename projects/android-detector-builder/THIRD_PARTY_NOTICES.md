# Third-party components

The original detector template is based on [ncnn-android-yolov8 by nihui](https://github.com/nihui/ncnn-android-yolov8) and uses [Tencent NCNN](https://github.com/Tencent/ncnn) and [OpenCV Mobile](https://github.com/nihui/opencv-mobile). Obtain the template, libraries, and model weights separately under their respective terms.
